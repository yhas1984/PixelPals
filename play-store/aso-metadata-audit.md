# ASO metadata audit

PixelPals uses three explicit Google Play locales:

- `es-419`: neutral Latin American Spanish.
- `es-ES`: Spanish for Spain.
- `en-US`: American English.

The title leads with the brand and the primary discovery phrase (`mascota
virtual` / `virtual pet`). The short description uses an action-led adoption
and care promise, includes the home and on-screen differentiators, and stays
within Google's 80-character limit. Full descriptions introduce the primary
phrase naturally in the opening sentence, cover all 15 pets, and retain the
local-first, permissions, advertising, and purchase disclosures.

The two Spanish locales currently share the same visual assets. Their text is
localized independently so screenshots can be split later without blocking
the metadata correction. No experiment is scheduled until traffic is high
enough for Play Console's estimator to complete one within six weeks.
