# PixelPals Google Play preview video

The approved Play preview is the single shared file:

- `exports/pixelpals-play-store-preview.mp4`

Use this same video for the Spanish and English store listings. It is the
complete user-approved Higgsfield celebration video, including its original
audio. No localized composite, replacement soundtrack or additional edit is
used in the final deliverable.

The video is 10 seconds long, portrait 9:16, H.264/yuv420p with AAC stereo
audio. Run the repository validator before upload:

```bash
./tools/build_play_store_video.sh
```

The older screen recordings and ensemble still are retained only as source
provenance. They are not final preview videos and must not be uploaded to Play
Console or YouTube.

## AI-assisted footage

The final preview is AI-generated promotional footage produced with
Higgsfield. It is promotional art, not an app screenshot or a literal recording
of PixelPals gameplay. Declare AI assistance wherever the Play Console or the
video host requests it.

## Publishing checklist

- Upload `exports/pixelpals-play-store-preview.mp4` to YouTube as public or
  unlisted.
- Disable monetization and ads for the video.
- Keep embedding enabled and do not age-restrict it.
- Use the same full YouTube URL in both localized Play listings.
- Review both Play Console previews before submitting the listing.
- Declare that the celebration footage is AI-generated promotional media.
