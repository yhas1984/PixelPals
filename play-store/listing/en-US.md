PixelPals turns your phone into the home of a virtual companion with a personality of its own. Meet 15 pets, discover what makes each one unique, care for them at home and, if you choose, bring them with you over other apps.

A COMPANION THAT FEELS ALIVE

Every pet has its own temperament, pace, favorite toy and way of reacting. Your bond grows through the moments you share and changes their greetings, trust and initiative. Feed, play, clean, help them rest and show affection through care designed for each species.

A HOME TO MAKE YOUR OWN

Create a welcoming space with beds, toys, bowls, plants and displays. Place objects with your finger, save each layout and discover environments built around every companion's nature. Your pet can rest, play and approach favorite objects on its own.

ADVENTURES, TREASURES AND MEMORIES

Go on expeditions to the meadow, forest or night sky. Return with treasures, decorations and new memories. The journal keeps adoption, care, play, discoveries and bond milestones together. You can also create static postcards and share them from Android.

15 PERSONALITIES TO MEET

From Corgi's loyal energy to Yuki's cheerful chill, Ginger's climbing curiosity, Tela's web or Bloop's ghostly flight, every companion has movement, care, a home and objects that fit its species.

ALWAYS WITH YOU, WHEN YOU WANT

The optional overlay lets your pet walk, rest and react over other apps. PixelPals remains fully usable inside the home without granting this permission. You can control sound, vibration, notifications and reduced motion.

SHOP AND PERSONALIZATION

Preview pets, cosmetics and decorations before choosing. Earn coins by playing and caring for your companion, unlock items through your bond and restore compatible purchases made through Google Play. The app may show ads and offer in-app purchases; there are no subscriptions or expedition speed-ups.

LOCAL AND ACCOUNT-FREE

Your progress, homes, memories and preferences stay on your device. No account or personal analytics is required. Notification access can provide care reminders; usage access improves optional behavior over other apps. PixelPals explains every permission in context, and you can deny or revoke it through Android.

Choose a companion, prepare a home and let a little personality come alive with you every day.
