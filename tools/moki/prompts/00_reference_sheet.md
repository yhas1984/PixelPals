# Moki — Character Reference Sheet (Storyboard Grid)

## Preflight Checkpoint
- **Variant title:** Moki Reference Sheet — mint teal chameleon v1
- **Viewer question:** "¿Qué mascota es Moki y cómo se distingue de las otras?"
- **Panel 1 job:** Frente limpio que define silueta, ojos ámbar y patas adhesivas.
- **Panel 2-3 support:** 3/4 y perfil lateral fijan volumen y cola en espiral.
- **Panel 4-6 progression:** Espalda + expresiones (curioso, contento, asustado, camuflaje).
- **Product reveal timing:** La cola en espiral debe leerse desde el panel 1.
- **Main drift risks:** Cambiar a lagartija alargada, perder la cola, añadir alas/cuernos, hornear texto en la imagen.

## Grid Spec
- **boardLayoutRatio:** 1:1 (1024x1024)
- **targetFrameAspectRatio:** 1:1 por celda (4 columnas x 2 filas)
- **Celdas:** 8

## Common Prompt (corto)
Soft pastel sticker illustration, polished clean edges, transparent-friendly flat light background (#F4F9F8), no text, no watermark, no UI, no letters, no symbols, no grid lines visible. Consistent character identity across all cells: same mint/teal chameleon, same proportions, same coral spots, same amber eyes.

## Panel Sequence (detallado)

### Panel 1 — Frente (identidad)
Front view, centered, full body visible, small mint chameleon standing upright facing camera, rounded triangular head, two large amber eyes on small eye turrets looking forward, body in mint #9FE8D9 with subtle teal shading #56C9C0 and deep teal underbelly #2E8F91, three small coral spots #FF8A7A on the side, curled spiral tail visible on the right side of the body, four small adhesive feet with tiny round toe pads, cream highlight #FFF6E8 on the head, soft warm outline #3B4C52, gentle specular sheen on scales, cute and sleek not fluffy.

### Panel 2 — Tres cuartos (volumen)
Three-quarter front view from the left, same chameleon, head turned slightly right, one eye ahead and one eye scanning sideways (independent eyes), tail curl clearly wrapping behind the body, body leans slightly forward, feet planted on the ground plane, same palette and materials.

### Panel 3 — Perfil lateral (silueta)
Side profile facing left, full silhouette of the body with a smooth rounded back, long spiral tail coiled at the end, four feet visible in side view, amber eye protruding from the turret, coral spots along the flank, cream highlight along the spine, same palette, ground shadow omitted or very soft.

### Panel 4 — Espalda y cola (lectura de cola)
Rear three-quarter view, back of the head with both eye turrets visible from behind, body angled to show the full spiral tail shape, feet gripping the ground, coral spots on the back, same palette and outline.

### Panel 5 — Expresión curiosa
Front view, head tilted slightly, both eyes wide with bright amber iris and small highlights, mouth slightly open as a tiny smile, tongue tip barely visible, tail raised slightly in a happy curl, same palette.

### Panel 6 — Expresión contenta
Front view, eyes relaxed with curved happy lids, tiny closed-mouth smile, body slightly puffed, tail loosely curled, coral spots visible, same palette, calm pose.

### Panel 7 — Expresión asustada / alerta
Front view, both eyes very wide with tiny pupils, mouth open in a small oval, body lowered toward the ground, tail curled tight around the body, one front foot lifted, same palette.

### Panel 8 — Camuflaje (referencia de tono)
Front view, same chameleon with body color shifting toward pale sage #D8E8E2 to match the flat background, spots faded, but eyes still amber and fully visible, subtle outline, clearly readable as the same character.

## Forbidden Drift
- No wings, no halo, no horns, no ears, no beak, no fluff.
- No long lizard neck or snake-like body.
- No text, letters, numbers, watermarks or annotations.
- No background props, no shadows that detach from the ground line.
- Do not change body proportions between cells.
- Keep the tail spiral in every cell.

## Product Reveal Timing
Panel 1 and 4 must show the spiral tail as the signature hook; panels 5-7 sell the personality; panel 8 establishes the camouflage ability reference.

image-generation (gpt-image-2) — then cleanup with transparency conversion.
