# Ginger v2 Storyboard

## Identity

- Orange mackerel tabby with six dark cinnamon stripes.
- Cream muzzle, chest, four sock paws, and tail tip.
- Sea-green eyes and a narrow teal collar with a round gold tag.
- Soft pastel storybook illustration; no pixel art or 3D rendering.
