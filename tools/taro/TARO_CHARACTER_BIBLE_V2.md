# Taro Character Bible v2

## Identity

Taro is a cheerful baby sea turtle with a calm, patient garden personality.
The canonical reference is `/home/yhas/Pictures/pixelpals_refs/taro.png`.

Required features in every frame:

- Light green scaled skin and rounded baby proportions.
- Large glossy dark eyes with teal and blue highlights.
- Rosy cheeks and a friendly open smile when the expression calls for it.
- Pale yellow segmented plastron on the front.
- Green patterned carapace with a warm orange rim.
- Rounded flippers with readable cream claw tips.

## Motion Language

- Taro moves slowly and deliberately, never like a fish or a fast runner.
- Feet stay on a stable ground line during walk, hide, peek, and curiosity clips.
- The shell remains the same size and shape across adjacent poses.
- Hiding is a readable emotional reaction, not a shell transformation.
- Sleep is a compact grounded hold with subtle breathing.
- Front social frames must preserve the plastron and both shell edges.

## Rejection Gates

Reject any frame with identity drift, changed shell colors, missing flippers or
claws, duplicated limbs, cropped shell, floating feet, unstable scale, white
matte, labels, scenery, detached shadows, or a pose that reads as swimming.
