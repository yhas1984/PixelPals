# Corgi turn board

Mode: built-in image generation with original corgi_0.png as identity and style reference. Output is a candidate until extracted, calibrated and visually reviewed.

Use case: identity-preserve. Production animation artwork for PixelPals, NOT a redesign. The supplied image is the identity and style reference: the exact existing original orange-and-cream Corgi, happy open mouth with pink tongue, big upright ears, dark brown outlines, soft painted fur, very short legs and long low torso.
Create ONE precise four-panel animation drawing board, a 2 by 2 grid of equal square cells, read left to right then top to bottom. NO visible panel borders, labels, text, ground lines, shadows, effects or props. Exactly one complete dog in each cell. All four cells share one fixed orthographic game camera, identical character anatomy and body dimensions in world space. Character fills about 80 percent of each cell height, with paws at a common y about 91 percent. Empty space stays empty; don't magnify the narrower front-facing dog.
This is a small, physically plausible standing turn from the reference's right-facing three-quarter direction toward the viewer. The dog stays STANDING on its four short legs throughout, with hips raised at their normal standing height; it never sits or crouches.
Cell 1 top-left: copy the reference's exact original standing pose, proportions, rightward three-quarter orientation, expression and palette. This is the calibration reference.
Cell 2 top-right: first pivot step, about 45 degrees to the right of a direct front view. Eyes and nose look toward the viewer a little before the shoulders follow. One forepaw has just been repositioned toward the new heading; the other supports weight. Hindquarters follow the turn, no sliding legs.
Cell 3 bottom-left: second pivot step, about 20 degrees to the right of direct front. Torso now more front-facing, all four short legs are still anatomically accounted for (rear paws may be partly occluded), forepaws settle under the shoulders. Head size, ear length, muzzle dimensions, chest and foreleg length are exactly consistent with cells 1 and 2.
Cell 4 bottom-right: full direct FRONT VIEW, a standing endpoint with the front paws spaced beneath the shoulders, rear legs visibly supporting the rump behind them where perspective permits. Same happy tongue-out face and upright ears. Body width is naturally narrower due to the turn; NEVER compensate with camera zoom or enlarge the head. Tail follows behind, with its orange-and-cream tip visible at a side.
Keep the same fine original outline weight, orange hue, cream chest, central white face blaze, glossy eyes, eyebrow spots and soft painted fur detail across all cells. No chromatic aberration, rainbow edges, scan lines, duplicate legs, sitting pose, elongated legs, different dog, altered facial expression or floating paw held unnaturally. Smooth logical weight transfer, grounded and affectionate character. Genuine transparent RGBA background outside all dogs. No checkerboard painted into pixels; no background scenery. Preserve fur detail and fine outline without halos.
